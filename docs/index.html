What to keep in mind

The real policy rate here uses inflation over the past year, not what people expect inflation to be. It says where policy sits against recent prices, not whether it is too tight or too loose.

The rate change chart only has the days the policy rate moved. Holds are not in the data. Most changes were expected by markets ahead of time, so a small move in the window does not mean the decision did not matter, and other news lands in the same few days. I read it as a description, not a causal estimate.

CPI and unemployment come out about three to seven weeks after the month ends, so they lag the daily series. CPI is not seasonally adjusted, which is fine for the year over year change used here.
